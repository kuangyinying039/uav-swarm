# Dual-pursuit difficulty ladder

Baseline: **`hard`**. `building_count` is **5** on every level.

`nominal` / `medium` are strictly easier than `hard` on the axes below.
`severe` / `extreme` keep the historical configs (unchanged from the pre-ladder edit).

| Axis | nominal | medium | hard | severe | extreme |
|------|--------:|-------:|-----:|-------:|--------:|
| `building_count` | 5 | 5 | **5** | 5 | 5 |
| `target_speed` | 1.40 | 1.46 | **1.52** | 1.72 | 1.76 |
| `target_vertical_speed` | 0.80 | 0.84 | **0.88** | 1.00 | 1.02 |
| `building_min/max_size` | 2.0–4.0 | 2.5–4.5 | **3.0–5.0** | 3.25–5.75 | 3.32–5.82 |
| `building_min/max_height` | 3.0–9.0 | 4.0–10.5 | **5.0–11.5** | 5.5–11.75 | 5.6–11.82 |
| `handoff_formation_*` | 5–7 | 6.5–8.5 | **8–10** | 10–12 | 10.25–12.25 |
| `lidar_target_detection_range` | 15 | 14 | **13** | 11 | 10.5 |
| `lidar_detection_probability` | 0.95 | 0.92 | **0.88** | 0.80 | 0.80 |
